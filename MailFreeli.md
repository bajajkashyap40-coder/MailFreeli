# 🚀 MailFreeli
> **AI-Driven Email Dispatcher**

MailFreeli is an intelligent, high-velocity email generation and delivery platform designed to eliminate manual email drafting. Powered by Groq (Llama-3) and built on the MERN stack, MailFreeli converts raw prompts into structured, polished email subjects and bodies, dispatching them instantly via SMTP.

---

## 🎯 Value Proposition & Consumer Benefits

* **Zero-Friction Drafting:** Eliminates writer’s block by generating complete, professionally styled emails from 1-line prompts.
* **Instant Delivery:** Real-time generation and automated dispatch using high-speed Groq inference and Nodemailer transport.
* **Audit Trail & Tracking:** Persists recipient, prompt, AI output, and dispatch status in MongoDB Atlas for history and auditing.
